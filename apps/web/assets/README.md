# Personajes de la mesa

La mesa principal ya usa modelos geométricos 3D de los diez personajes (ver `design/ESCENA_3D.md`). El atlas de esta carpeta se conserva para la galería y la alternativa sencilla cuando WebGL no está disponible. Las caras de las cartas proceden de los dibujos originales de `card-art.js`, compartidos con las texturas 3D.

`elteto-mascots-v1.png`: atlas RGBA de 1536 × 1024, diez personajes sentados en una cuadrícula de 5 × 2. Creado con la herramienta de generación de imágenes a partir de las referencias aportadas por el usuario (conservadas en `design/references/`). Se muestra mediante CSS; no depende de servidores externos y se incluye en la caché sin conexión y en Android LAN.

Orden: Berenjena, Melocotón, Plátano, Bandera, Aguacate, Cerdito, Seta, Gota, Caca y Castañas. La selección única se deriva del listado público de jugadores y del asiento original; todos los dispositivos ven la misma identidad, aunque miren desde posiciones diferentes. La portada permite ver los diez personajes.

Las ilustraciones tienen volumen, silla de madera y tapicería verde, respiración suave y reacciones al actuar o recibir el turno. No son modelos articulados ni una simulación física 3D. El abanico superpuesto utiliza exclusivamente el número público real de cartas; nunca recibe los valores rivales. Las vistas de tablero sin `handSizes` dejan las manos vacías. El movimiento se desactiva con la preferencia de movimiento reducido. Para sustituir el atlas se debe conservar la distribución 5 × 2, proporción de celda 3:5 y márgenes. `table-players-v1.png` es el recurso humano anterior, ya sin uso en la mesa.

## Prompt del atlas actual

Create ONE production game sprite atlas on TRUE TRANSPARENT background. Exactly 5 equal columns by 2 equal rows, no gutters, each sprite centered entirely within its equal cell, generous transparent margin so none overlaps. Preserve exactly the TEN character identities and order from reference character sheet. Row1: purple grumpy eggplant green calyx; orange flirty peach green leaves; yellow banana brown cowboy sheriff hat; smiling Japanese flag character pole/gloves/boots; winking green avocado brown pit silver hoop earring. Row2: pink pig snout character; red cream-spotted mushroom with cream face and cap puffs; angry blue water drop; brown poop swirl sunglasses gold dollar chain; pair of brown chestnuts with orange impact tuft. NO text, NO numbers, NO logos, NO colored backgrounds, NO humans. Adapt each into the seated opponent pose of the table mockups: colorful glossy cartoon 3D-like illustration with bold black outlines, warm highlights and rich shading. Each sits in a rich brown carved wooden armchair with dark green upholstered back. Camera frontal slightly elevated, upper body prominent; forearms resting forward at waist with both hands visible, ready to hold cards added by the game. Empty hands: DO NOT DRAW CARDS or table. Chair base visible, same composition scale for each sprite, head near cell top with breathing room, hands at 65% cell height, chair feet at bottom. Characters must match the distinctive faces/accessories of reference, not generic fruit. Overall atlas landscape 3:2 approximate, clean alpha edges.

## Prompt histórico del recurso humano anterior

+Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production character atlas for a mobile multiplayer card game, transparent PNG. Create ONE clean sprite sheet in a strict 3-column by 2-row grid, six equal square cells, landscape aspect ratio 3:2. Every character is isolated on genuine transparency, no background, no table, no labels, no cell borders. Each cell contains exactly one distinct adult human seated at a card table, visible from head to waist with both shoulders, elbows, forearms and hands fully visible. Polished warm 3D videogame character renders, convincing rounded volume, cloth, skin, hair and soft cinematic light, mature friendly playful card-room style. Camera front-facing and slightly above, as viewed by the opposite player across a table. Characters gaze down toward the playing cards held in their hands at chest/desk height; both arms extend forward and elbows look naturally rested on an invisible tabletop. Each holds a modest fan of exactly FOUR playing cards with ornate plum-purple and ivory backs facing the camera, fronts hidden. Six different recognisable people: top-left young dark-haired man with light stubble and navy rolled-sleeve shirt; top-middle woman with auburn bob and burgundy shirt; top-right middle-aged silver-haired man with glasses and forest-green shirt; bottom-left young woman with dark curly hair and ochre blouse; bottom-middle bald bearded man with teal rolled-sleeve shirt; bottom-right woman with dark ponytail and violet blouse. Consistent body scale, camera, framing, lighting and rendering across all six. Entire person silhouette contained in own cell, generous empty transparent margin 8 percent on each side, no overlaps between cells, no feet, no chairs visible. Center each figure in its cell with top of hair at 6 percent and waist at 92 percent cell height. Faces and hands anatomically clean, fingers grip card edges naturally. Card hands at 72 percent height of each cell, visibly in front of torso. No text, no typography, no watermark, no extra people.
